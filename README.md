🌸 Habit Tracker 

A cute, calm habit tracker planner that runs in your browser. All totals update on their own as you tick.

## Features : -

* Habits tab: a monthly grid with a tick circle for each day, plus percent done and best streak for every habit
* Auto summary: a monthly completion donut, week-by-week progress bars, longest streak, perfect days and total ticks
* Mood tab: tap a day to cycle through happy, calm, okay, sad and tense, with a count of each mood
* Routines tab: editable morning and evening checklists with a "Start a new day" reset
* Sleep tab: log hours for each day and see a bar chart and your average
* Month and year switcher: jump to any month of any year
* Light and dark theme: a round toggle button at the top right that remembers your choice
* Saves automatically: your data stays in your browser through localStorage
* Responsive: works on desktop and phone, and the habit grid scrolls sideways on small screens
